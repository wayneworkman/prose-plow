First release.

* Chat with small language models that are packaged in the app and run on the phone, with no network permission.
* Several chats; the model can be switched in the middle of a chat.
* Stop a reply, ask for an answer again, continue a reply, edit an earlier message.
* A temperature for each model, and a system prompt for the models trained with one.
* Chats are stored encrypted behind a mandatory lock: a passphrase or a fingerprint, never a PIN.
